U.S. Competitive Landscape

Confirmed U.S. operators and internationally based providers with documented U.S. availability or regulatory footing.

The simple rule

Website access is not authorization

A U.S.-accessible website or app does not establish nationwide licensing, escrow authority or broad customer eligibility.

Confirmed U.S. presence

Escrow.com

Release: 1999 Confirmed · broad
Primary focus

Traditional online escrow

Strengths

Long operating history, strong brand recognition and an extensive licensing footprint.

Target market

Domains, vehicles, business assets and other formal transactions

Weakness / opportunity

A formal, web-led process that is less intuitive than a consumer payment application.

U.S. footprint / regulatory basis

U.S.-based provider. Internet Escrow Services, Inc. maintains escrow or money-transmitter licences across most states; official materials also identify states where a licence is not required.

Trustap

Release: 2016 Confirmed · supported
Primary focus

Marketplace payment infrastructure and protected transactions

Strengths

Mature API, shipping integrations and established marketplace relationships.

Target market

P2P marketplaces, ecommerce, ticketing and specialist verticals

Weakness / opportunity

Increasingly oriented toward B2B infrastructure rather than a stand-alone consumer escrow brand.

U.S. footprint / regulatory basis

Ireland-incorporated company. Official country documentation lists the United States as a supported seller and registration country and supports U.S.-dollar transactions.

TRUST Escrow

New / emerging
Release: 2025 Confirmed · U.S.
Primary focus

Licensed digital escrow for goods and services

Strengths

California escrow licence, mobile application and segregated trust-account model.

Target market

U.S. consumers and businesses transacting in goods or services

Weakness / opportunity

A new entrant with limited visible adoption and a shorter operating history.

U.S. footprint / regulatory basis

California-headquartered, licensed and bonded under CA DFPI Licence No. 96DBO-188147. Its terms state that the platform is provided for users residing in the United States or its territories.

Tazapay

Release: 2020 Confirmed · limited
Primary focus

Cross-border payments, collections and escrow-style workflows

Strengths

Global payment rails, API infrastructure and multiple regulatory registrations.

Target market

International B2B ecommerce, SaaS providers and platforms

Weakness / opportunity

Enterprise and cross-border orientation rather than straightforward consumer P2P escrow.

U.S. footprint / regulatory basis

Tazapay US Corp. is FinCEN-registered and lists money-transmitter licences in Arizona, Arkansas, Illinois, Iowa, Michigan, Missouri and South Carolina; coverage is not nationwide.

Release: 2024 No U.S. operation verified

Mobile-first consumer escrow

Strengths

U.S. footprint / regulatory basis

Market implication

The confirmed U.S. set remains small and differentiated: no competitor combines broad U.S. coverage, simple consumer onboarding and a clearly mobile-first everyday escrow proposition.

Branding implication

Before investing in the MyEscrow name, complete formal trademark clearance and assess the discoverability impact of the similarly named myeskrow.com platform.
