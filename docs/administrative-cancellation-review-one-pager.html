Administrative Cancellation Review

A safety checkpoint for funded cancellation requests. Money stays protected while an admin checks the process and chooses the correct next step.

The simple rule

Traffic controller, not judge

The admin can ask, close, refer, or execute—but cannot decide whether the buyer or seller is right.

What the admin can do
1

Ask for information

Request missing facts or documents. The money remains held while the party responds.

Example
“Please provide the delivery date and cancellation notice.”
2

Close procedurally

Close a request that cannot be processed under the rules. This does not declare a winner.

Example
The same cancellation request was submitted twice.
3

Refer a milestone

Send a genuine disagreement into the formal dispute process. Only the selected funded milestone is reserved.

Example
The $500 homepage design enters dispute; other website milestones continue.
4

Execute a final decision

Carry out an existing final court order or arbitration award. The admin does not create the decision.

Example
A final award orders the exact $500 held to be refunded.

Where the line is

Administrative review is not arbitration

Admin manages the processChecks rules, gathers information, routes the case, and records every action.
Arbitrator decides the disputeReviews evidence, decides the merits, and may issue a binding award.
RationaleWhy the admin is taking the selected action.
Policy or milestoneThe rule used or the exact funded milestone referred.
Final authorityOrder or award type, reference, and effective date.
Document fingerprintSHA-256 identifies the retained copy; it does not replace storing it.
