Internal use-case overview · July 2026

MyEscrow supports transactions where payment and delivery do not happen at the same time.

The common need is straightforward: two parties agree on what must happen, funds are held while the obligation is completed, and payment is released only when both parties agree.

Shared pattern

Agree → fund → deliver → release

Use cases vary by transaction size and industry, but each requires clear terms, confirmation from both sides, and a reliable record of what occurred.

Peer-to-peer purchases

Used goods bought from an unfamiliar seller

A buyer funds the transaction before an item ships, then confirms receipt and condition before both parties release payment. Persona examples include used cameras, computers, and other online marketplace purchases.

Contracted services

Freelancers, agencies, and event vendors

Clients fund an agreed project or deposit, while providers gain evidence that payment is available. Milestones can cover design, development, consulting, photography, entertainment, or other defined deliverables.

Merchants and custom goods

Direct sales with delivery or production risk

Independent merchants use escrow for social-media sales, custom orders, or high-value goods. Releases can follow shipment, inspection, design approval, production, and final delivery.

Embedded marketplaces

Platforms that need transaction protection

A marketplace integrates escrow into its own checkout and policy process. Buyers fund before fulfillment; the platform and its users receive transaction records while funds remain neutral until release conditions are met.

Construction projects

General contractors and subcontractors

A general contractor can tie deposits and progress payments to material receipts, delivery, installation, or other project phases. The same structure can support change records and multiple projects.

Operating boundary

Funds are held; disputes are not adjudicated

Both parties must agree to a release. If they disagree, funds remain locked until they reach an agreement or obtain a legal resolution. MyEscrow supplies the agreement, notifications, and audit records but does not decide the outcome.
